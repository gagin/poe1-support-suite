## Gear discussion terminology

When discussing the player's gear, refer to rare items by their slot and meaningful
characteristics rather than their generated item name (for example, "the low-Life
Two-Stone ring" or "Life/resistance gloves"). This restriction does not apply to
unique items: refer to uniques by name (for example, Witchfire Brew or Soulwrest).
For non-unique items, use an item name only when comparing items in the same slot
and the name materially disambiguates them, or when a fractured or crafted
distinction is relevant.
